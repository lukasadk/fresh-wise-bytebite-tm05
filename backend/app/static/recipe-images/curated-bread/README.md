# Curated bread and milk recipe images

These local 3:2 JPEG derivatives are used only for the matching finished dish.
They were cropped, resized, lightly colour/contrast adjusted, and compressed by
FreshWise for mobile display. Copyright, author, source-page, licence, and
modification details for every image are recorded in:

`backend/data/recipe_rag/recipe_image_sources.json`

The images come from Wikimedia Commons. CC BY and CC BY-SA works retain their
file-specific Creative Commons licence; CC0 works remain available under CC0.
Do not separate the images from the attribution catalogue when redistributing
the app or backend bundle.

The runtime matching groups are:

- Roti John
- Kaya toast / kaya bread
- Milk or condensed-milk toast
- French toast
- Bread pudding
- Stuffed roti canai / murtabak, including a sardine-specific image
- Ramly-style burgers, including a beef-specific image
